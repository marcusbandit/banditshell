# The 2026-10-17 audit and what the branch did about it

Branch `full-system-audit-roadmap`. Nothing here is on main. Six read-only
audits ran first (theming, architecture, the CLI and binds, the surfaces
around notifications, wiring, ideas from other shells); their full reports
stay out of the repo. This file is what they found that matters, what the
branch changed, and what is left.

## What the audits found

- Two hubs held the shell together. `modules/ShellWindow.qml` named every
  panel eight to eleven times, and `modules/Cli.qml` held all 33 IPC
  handlers. The launcher reached 101 of 110 services. Five import cycles
  blocked any clean split, and seven services imported code from `modules/`.
- Binds were CLI strings. The CLI was regrouped twice in two weeks; each
  time, binds broke and a regex migration tried to rewrite people's
  Hyprland files after the fact.
- Updates merged straight into the live checkout and trusted hot reload,
  which misses new qmldir entries. There was no health check, no real
  rollback, and nothing told the user what kind of change was coming.
- Notification history lived only in memory, so every reload emptied it.
  Pressing a card in the corner tray deleted it instead of opening the app.
  No card showed when it arrived, the "lock" rule column did nothing, inline
  reply was never offered, and three different card layouts drew one
  notification.
- About 184 animations ran outside `Follow`, eight eased with an overshoot,
  seven `Rectangle` radii were left, the lock screen had two extra font
  sizes, and 49 colours were hex literals outside the palette.
- A dead verb (`panel settings float`), every IPC failure reported as "not
  running", a missing `theme-set` that nothing checked for, and runtime
  state written into `config.json`.

## What the branch changed

### Parts that can ship apart

- Every IPC handler lives next to its module (`modules/<name>/<Name>Ipc.qml`,
  `modules/ipc/` for services), listed once in `modules/Ipc.qml`. A snapshot
  test holds every target and signature still.
- The panel contract has its own folder, `modules/panel/`.
- 38 packages each declare what they own and need, in `module.json`
  manifests. `scripts/check-modules.js` (`banditshell dev modules`) fails on
  any new boundary crossing; the 425 that exist today are a baseline that has
  only gone down since (now 404).
- Five interface singletons in core replace upward reaches: `SessionControl`
  (lock now), `Notify` (post an alert), `Focus` (which screen is off stage),
  `Panels` (open a panel by name), `System` (home, copy, open, reveal).
- No service imports from `modules/` any more, and components take their
  tuning as properties instead of reading config.

See `docs/architecture/modules.md` and `docs/architecture/centre.md`.

### Binds that survive CLI changes

- `api/actions.tsv` is the contract: 112 stable action ids such as
  `launcher.toggle` or `panel.clipboard.toggle`. An id is never deleted.
- `banditshell action <id>` resolves an id at the moment it runs, so an
  update that lands mid-session cannot break a loaded config.
- `lua/banditshell.lua`, loaded through a frozen shim beside
  `hyprland.lua`: `hl.bind("SUPER + SPACE", bs.launcher.toggle)`. Unknown or
  removed names never raise; they notify once. The launcher toggle
  dispatches the shell's global shortcut directly when Hyprland offers it.
- `banditshell version`, `api list|show|check`, exit codes (0 ok, 1 failed,
  2 usage, 3 not running, 4 refused), `--json` on every status, a cached pid
  instead of `qs list` on every call, `doctor --report`, and an event
  stream: `banditshell events`.

### Updates for people who did not write the shell

`banditshell update check|apply|rollback|status`. A check says what is
coming before anything downloads (restart or reload, commands changed,
dependencies). Apply stages the new tree and compile-checks it before the
live checkout moves, restarts only when the change needs it, and a health
check after the restart rolls back on its own and skips that version. There
are `stable` and `main` channels (default `main`, so nothing changes until a
copy opts in), version numbers taken from the What's new ids, and
`scripts/promote-stable.sh`, which prints the push but never runs it. See
`docs/architecture/updates.md`.

### Notifications

Live notifications live in the island; the corner tray is gone (a slim
live-only host remains for `placement: corner`). History is a launcher mode:
`notifications`, `unread`, `held`, `from <app>` and free text, one row per
thread, with Reply, Snooze, Mute, Copy and Dismiss, and the full card beside
the launcher. History survives restarts, cards show their age, a press opens
the app, critical notifications pass quiet hours, quiet shows in the sidebar
and the island, and one card anatomy is shared by the island, the launcher
and the lock screen. `banditshell notifications ...` drives all of it.

### The island

An activity registry any script can post to (`banditshell island push --id
build --text Building --progress 0.4`, then `update` and `end`). Timers with
a ring that take the big spot in their last seconds, alarms with Snooze and
Stop, volume, brightness and mic moments, the screenshot just taken with
Copy, Edit, Folder and Delete, privacy dots for the mic, camera and screen
share, "Copied", and headphones connecting with their battery.

### The sidebar

A `rail` style (`sidebar.style`): workspace pips at rest, a status icon only
when something is off (low battery, VPN, mic live, recording, quiet, an
update), and the full column grows out of the band on hover. Scroll on the
sound icon sets the volume. The full style is unchanged.

### The launcher

New sources, added without touching the launcher's core: `keys <words>`
(which chord does it), `tray`, `scratchpad` and `special <name>`, `action
<words>` (every stable id), `recent` documents, and the notification history.

### Theming and the house rules

Motion goes through `Follow` and a new `FollowColour` everywhere outside the
launcher, with no overshoot. New tokens (`padding.hair`, opacity, stroke,
icon ladder, `colour.pane`), one state recipe for rest, hover, pressed,
selected, disabled and focus, and colour maths through Appearance helpers.
`scripts/lint-rules.js` (`banditshell dev lint`) encodes the rules from
DESIGN.md and CLAUDE.md as L1 to L11, with a baseline that only shrinks.
What is left in it is in the launcher, which is developed on its own branch.
L11 catches a read of a singleton member that does not exist, the kind of
bug that compiles and is `undefined` at runtime.

## What is left

1. The `Panels` registry, so `ShellWindow` builds panels from a list instead
   of importing them (architecture D4, step 10), then `Menus`, `Bands` and
   `SettingsPages`.
2. `scripts/pack-module.js`: copy one package and what it needs into its
   own tree, the first real "ship the launcher alone".
3. The launcher's own motion and colour leftovers (34 and 17 lint entries).
4. Wiring the stable-id bind migration into the update offer, so old binds
   are rewritten to `bs.<id>` with one press.
5. Pills (drawn two ways today) and the Material 3 leftovers in Button,
   Slider, Toggle and the lock's password marks.
6. Ideas not yet built, in order: calendar events in the launcher and the
   island, a hold-to-cycle window switcher, AT-SPI accessibility, theme
   templates for other apps, and a contrast check across wallpapers.
